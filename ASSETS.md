# Forest graphics

The day clearing, matching StarFern clearing, and transparent Mossling were created for this project with the built-in image generation tool. They are optimized as WebP for the website; original generation files are preserved separately. There are no downloaded third-party images in this release.

- `assets/day-canopy.webp`: a panoramic, tactile alien fairytale clearing with cream sunlight, moss, hanging vines, pearlescent flowers, a stream, organic arches, and distant floating islands. The center is calm enough for clear content panels.
- `assets/starfern-forest.webp`: the same composition edited to moonlit indigo, evergreen, silver, and lavender, with glowing flowers and mushrooms. Terrain, tree positions, and stream placement remain consistent.
- `assets/mossling.webp`: a transparent, plush ivory creature with folded fern ears, hazel eyes, cream flowers, dew, and a tiny floating seed. It is rendered over the real page background.

## Generation briefs

Day environment: sophisticated cozy forest learning website backdrop; detailed tactile 3D illustration; calm open center two thirds for glass content; cream, oatmeal, moss; vines and trees frame edges, flowers and ferns below; shallow stream, stones, distant organic future arches and islands; no UI, text, logos, or focal creatures.

Night environment edit: preserve the exact scene composition and change lighting/atmosphere/plant glow only; deep indigo, blue-gray, evergreen, lavender, moonlit silver, small moon, bioluminescent edge plants, stars and fireflies; crisp visible forest detail, no fog, no text or UI.

Mossling: a single full-body transparent creature cutout, round ivory/oatmeal plush fur, long folded moss-green fern ears, pearly hazel eyes, tiny paws and a raised paw near a floating seed; delicate flowers and dew, gentle alien fairytale design, tactile 3D materials; no ground rectangle, text, clothing, or other props.
