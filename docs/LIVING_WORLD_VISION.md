# Lumen & Loam — a living study world

Nova's direction, November 9, 2026. These are future ideas, not shipped features or a fixed implementation schedule.

## The feeling
A useful study space that grows into an explorable world. Inspirations: lofi.co's study atmosphere, Forest's growing gardens, old-school Neopets' places/shops/random events, Petz (Dogz and Catz 3–5)'s personal creatures, and Fortnite's getting-started quests. Keep adding discoveries while real classes remain usable.

## Places as little worlds
Secrets and games continue to unlock places. A location can eventually bring its own creature teachers, games, lore, music/soundscapes, shop, plants, flowers, trees, decorations, and possibilities we have not imagined yet. Once collected, it remains selectable. Before discovery, hide its name and picture; a tiny fallen petal or snowflake should invite curiosity.

## Focus gardens
Grow different plants through study/focus sessions. View today's garden and also the week's and month's gardens. Different places can offer their own species. Explore a full app and distraction-blocking capabilities later, including what each platform actually allows.

## Quests, currency, and shops
A friendly getting-started quest chain teaches navigation through small actions: start a class, add an initial task, try useful study features, and notice a hint toward a hidden game. Award a few coins or points per quest. Games and other tasks could also earn currency. Each place has a shop with its own creatures, plants, flowers, trees, and cute things to collect. Choose the currency name, rewards, prices, and save model in a later playable slice.

## Personal creatures
A nursery where learners hatch their own pets, care for them, and play with them. Later explore breeding, inherited variations, and sharing bred pets. Preserve the affectionate, slightly silly Petz feeling; creature teachers and companions can connect to newly discovered places.

## Random forest events and a bank
Occasional surprises: finding coins, a rare item, a helpful encounter, or a mischievous setback. Nova also proposed a bank and the possibility of loose coins being stolen if they are not stored. Prototype event frequency, consequences, and recovery before choosing the final rules.

## A connected future
Eventually: real accounts, sharing classes, sharing pets, branches/connections between different people's classes, and an AI built into the site. Each needs a separate working design and real infrastructure; the current site saves individual progress in the browser with JSON backups.

## Build in small, playable additions
The current foundation has classes, independent study tools, saved discoveries, games, collectible places, and matching creatures. Possible next slices: an onboarding quest journal; one earned currency and a tiny shop; a plant grown from a completed focus session; one hatchable companion. Choose a slice with Nova when ready instead of treating this brainstorm as permission to ship all of them at once.
